Blood Group Compatibility

Understand which blood groups match for safe, life-saving transfusions.

O- Universal Donor

Can give to: All Blood Types (A+, A-, B+, B-, O+, O-, AB+, AB-)

Can receive from: O- only

O+ Most Common

Can give to: O+, A+, B+, AB+

Can receive from: O+, O-

AB+ Universal Recipient

Can give to: AB+ only

Can receive from: All Blood Types

A+ / B+ High Demand

A+ gives to: A+, AB+ | Receives: A+, A-, O+, O-
