How to Use RedPulse

1. Register as a Donor

To become a volunteer donor, click the "Be a Donor" button on the top right. Fill in your details including your blood group, city, and phone number. This helps us match you with emergency requests in your area.

2. Finding Donors

If you need to find donors, go to the "Find Donors" page. You can filter by blood group, city, or availability to quickly find someone who matches your requirements.

3. Posting Blood Requests

If you or someone else needs blood, click "Need Blood?" in the header. Submit a request with patient details, hospital location, and urgency level. Once posted, donors in the system can view your request.

4. Manage Your Profile

Once logged in, your "Dashboard" allows you to manage your donor profile, update your availability status, and track requests you've posted.
